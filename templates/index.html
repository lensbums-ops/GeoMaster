How to play
🗺️ Each round one player picks a clue type. Everyone guesses simultaneously by clicking the correct country or city on the map.
📍 The closer your pin to the target, the more points you earn. A perfect hit scores 5,000 pts. Over 10,000 km away scores 0.
⏱️ You have 30 seconds per round. A placed marker is auto-confirmed when time runs out; no marker means a phantom in the ocean and a 500 pt penalty.
🔥 Streak bonus: 3 rounds under 1,000 km in a row earns +500 pts and sets you on fire.
🃏 Jokers: each player has two: Double Points (×2 your score) and Peek (see the target for 3 seconds). Use them wisely, once per game each.
🏆 After all rounds the player with the most points wins. Scores are hidden until the reveal.
